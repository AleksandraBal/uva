From saying to doing

AI says something wrong

AI does something wrong

An agent sends the email, updates the record, submits the filing.

Three failure modes: Excessive permissions · Unclear scope · Silent failure

Failure mode 1: Excessive permissions

You asked for a draft. It sent the message.

How to prevent it

Least privilege: draft-only unless sending is the task

A separate identity for each agent, not your login

Human approval for the exact action: this message, this filing

4

Failure mode 2: Unclear scope

A customer support bot treated a missing feature as a bug.

Your tax engine got this wrong.
You’re right to criticise them.

Product limitations: not read

Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149: a company is responsible for what its chatbot tells customers.

How to prevent it

Give the agent access to the right, up-to-date sources, and make it consult them

Define what the agent must not say, not only what it should; hand the rest to a human

Test continuously: run the same hard questions many times, measure how often it fails, re-test after every change

5

Failure mode 3: Silent failure

The database was down. The agent carried on.

How to prevent it

The harness stops when a required step fails; the model doesn't decide

Log every action: trigger, what it read, tools called, errors, what it changed, who approved

Judge the path, not only the final answer
